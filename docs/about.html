About Us

Founded in Australia in 2026, KEMIZTECH is driven by a simple mission: to build thoughtful, high-performance software that bridges creative expression and everyday utility.

Started by Adam—an independent developer with over four years of dedicated R&D across Android and Windows ecosystems—our work is rooted in practical problem-solving and clean design. We believe that powerful technology should remain accessible, reliable, and genuinely easy to use.

What We Build

Our software ecosystem spans two primary pillars designed to enhance both creative workflows and daily productivity:

  • Art & Creativity: Specialized tools built to support digital artists, designers, and creators. From precise touch-handling and stylus support to vector line-art stencils and sketch software, our creative applications provide fluid environments for artistic exploration.
  • Everyday Utilities: Practical, high-efficiency tools designed to give you deeper control over your devices. Our file-management and system utilities make managing storage, browsing files, and organizing data across phones, memory cards, and PCs straightforward and frictionless.
