Camp Paths Privacy Policy

Effective 28 November 2026

KEMIZTECH publishes Camp Paths. The game does not require an account. Puzzle progress is stored on your device and can be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling the game.

Advertising

Camp Paths uses Google Mobile Ads to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. Google may collect and share your IP address, approximate location inferred from it, app and ad interactions, diagnostics such as crash logs, and device or account identifiers. Google Mobile Ads encrypts this data in transit. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy for more information about Google's processing and retention.

Your choices

Where available, you can review advertising consent in Levels → Privacy choices. Android also provides controls to reset or delete the advertising ID. Puzzle progress stays on the device until you remove it. We do not maintain a Camp Paths account or a server copy of your gameplay.
