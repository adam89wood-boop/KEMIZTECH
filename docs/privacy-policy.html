1. Information We Collect

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our service to you.

Log Files: www.kemiztech.com.au follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

Cookies and Web Beacons: Like any other website, www.kemiztech.com.au uses "cookies". These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

2. Neon Drop: Physics Arcade

Neon Drop: Physics Arcade is an offline physics arcade game for Android. You can play without creating an account. The app stores your current run, scores, quests, cosmetic settings and audio preferences locally on your device only; KEMIZTECH does not operate a game account database or a gameplay analytics server for this app.

The app offers one optional rewarded advertisement per run, provided by Google AdMob, that grants 25 additional in-game tokens. Starting a new run is always free without watching an ad, and these tokens have no monetary value. See "Google AdMob and Third-Party Ads" below for what Google and its advertising partners may collect when an ad is requested.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices before ads can be requested, and these choices can be revisited from the app's Settings menu. Neon Drop: Physics Arcade is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children.

3. Neon Blast: Grid Match

Neon Blast: Grid Match is an offline 8 × 8 block-puzzle game for Android. You can play without creating an account. The app stores your saved board, pieces, score and preferences locally on your device only; KEMIZTECH does not operate a game account database or a gameplay analytics server for this app.

The Google Play edition offers one optional rewarded video per game, provided by Google AdMob, that clears a small area of the board as a "rescue" when no more pieces fit. The Amazon Appstore edition offers the same optional rescue video through LevelPlay (Unity/ironSource) and its mediated ad networks instead of Google AdMob. Starting a new game is always free without watching a video. See "Google AdMob, LevelPlay (Unity/ironSource) and Third-Party Ads" below, and Unity's own privacy policy, for what these providers may collect when a video is requested.

The app also offers an optional one-time in-app purchase ("Neon Plus") that permanently removes ads and unlocks extra rescues and colour palettes. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play or the Amazon Appstore; we never receive, see, or store your payment card or account details, only confirmation of purchase and entitlement status, which we use to unlock the purchase and restore it on reinstall or on another device signed into the same store account.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform (Google edition) or LevelPlay's consent settings (Amazon edition) presents privacy choices before a video can be requested, and these choices can be revisited from the app's Settings menu. Neon Blast: Grid Match is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children.

www.kemiztech.com.au and KEMIZTECH's Android apps use Google AdMob, LevelPlay (Unity/ironSource) and/or other third-party advertising networks to serve ads.

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our app/website.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Alternatively, you can direct users to opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within our apps. AdMob may collect information such as your device's unique identifier (e.g., Android Advertising ID or IDFA), IP address, and app usage data to provide relevant ads.

Some of our Android apps (for example, the Amazon Appstore edition of Neon Blast: Grid Match) use LevelPlay (Unity/ironSource) and its mediated ad networks instead of Google AdMob to serve video and other ad formats. LevelPlay's use of device identifiers and other data for ads is governed by Unity's own privacy policy.

5. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the CCPA, among other rights, California consumers have the right to:

  • Request that a business that collects a consumer's personal data disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data that a business has collected about consumers.
  • Request that a business delete any personal data about the consumer that a business has collected.
  • Request that a business that sells a consumer's personal data, not sell the consumer's personal data.

If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us.
