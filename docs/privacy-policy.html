We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our service to you.

2. Neon Drop: Physics Arcade

Neon Drop: Physics Arcade is an offline physics arcade game for Android. You can play without creating an account. The app stores your current run, scores, quests, cosmetic settings and audio preferences locally on your device only; KEMIZTECH does not operate a game account database or a gameplay analytics server for this app.

The app offers one optional rewarded advertisement per run, provided by Google AdMob, that grants 25 additional in-game tokens. Starting a new run is always free without watching an ad, and these tokens have no monetary value. See "Google AdMob and Third-Party Ads" below for what Google and its advertising partners may collect when an ad is requested.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices before ads can be requested, and these choices can be revisited from the app's Settings menu. Neon Drop: Physics Arcade is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children.

3. Neon Blast: Grid Match

Neon Blast: Grid Match is an offline 8 × 8 block-puzzle game for Android. You can play without creating an account. The app stores your saved board, pieces, score and preferences locally on your device only; KEMIZTECH does not operate a game account database or a gameplay analytics server for this app.

Both the Google Play and Amazon Appstore editions use Google AdMob directly (not LevelPlay/Unity/ironSource) to show: an optional rewarded video per game that clears a small area of the board as a "rescue" when no more pieces fit; a small banner shown below the board during play; and an occasional full-screen interstitial, shown at most once every two "New game" restarts and at least 90 seconds apart. Starting a new game is always free without watching a video, and an unavailable or failed ad never blocks play. See "Google AdMob and Third-Party Ads" below for what Google and its advertising partners may collect when an ad is requested.

The app also offers an optional purchase ("Neon Plus") that permanently removes all ads and unlocks extra rescues and colour palettes, available either as a one-time lifetime purchase (Google Play and Amazon Appstore) or as an auto-renewing monthly subscription (Google Play only). Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play or the Amazon Appstore; we never receive, see, or store your payment card or account details, only confirmation of purchase/subscription status, which we use to unlock the purchase and restore it on reinstall, resubscription, or on another device signed into the same store account. All ads and the rescue video are automatically turned off for the duration of an active purchase or subscription.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices before ads can be requested, and these choices can be revisited from the app's Settings menu. Neon Blast: Grid Match is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children.

4. Keep My Word

Keep My Word is an offline speech-to-text app for Android (package com.kemtech.offlinetranscriptor). Speech recognition runs entirely on your device using a speech model you choose to download; your recordings, imported audio, transcripts, subtitle timings, edits and saved sessions are stored locally on your device only. KEMIZTECH does not operate a server that receives your audio, transcript text or filenames, and Android backup of the app's private data is disabled. Downloading a speech model requires an internet connection and contacts the model's host, Hugging Face (see Hugging Face's privacy policy); once a model is downloaded, recording and transcription work fully offline.

The free version of Keep My Word may show advertising, provided by Google AdMob, in the app's library/results screens after you have made a privacy/consent choice through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP); you can revisit that choice from the app's Privacy settings. No ad is shown while recording, importing or processing audio, and an unavailable ad or connection never blocks offline transcription. Completing an optional rewarded video grants 30 minutes without ads in the app's current session; it is not a purchase or a subscription and does not persist after the app is fully closed.

Keep My Word also offers an optional auto-renewing subscription that removes advertising, sold as a monthly or yearly plan through Google Play Billing (Google Play edition) or Amazon In-App Purchasing (Amazon Appstore edition). We never receive your payment card or store account details. To activate and keep your subscription recognised across reinstalls, the app sends your purchase/receipt token, the app's package name, a random installation identifier and, for Amazon purchases, an Amazon user identifier, to a KEMIZTECH-operated verification service. That service checks the receipt with Google Play or Amazon, and returns a signed, time-limited confirmation that advertising should stay off; it does not receive and cannot see your audio or transcript content, and logging of receipt tokens and user identifiers is disabled on that service. A verified subscription is cached on your device for up to 72 hours (some Amazon receipts refresh hourly) so the app keeps working ad-free offline between checks; transcription itself never depends on this service being reachable. See the Keep My Word subscription terms for billing details. Keep My Word is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children.

6. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the CCPA, among other rights, California consumers have the right to:

  • Request that a business that collects a consumer's personal data disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data that a business has collected about consumers.
  • Request that a business delete any personal data about the consumer that a business has collected.
  • Request that a business that sells a consumer's personal data, not sell the consumer's personal data.

If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us.
