Keep My Word — Subscription Terms

Effective Date: 19/11/2026

These terms cover the optional ad-free subscription inside Keep My Word (package com.kemtech.offlinetranscriptor), published by KEMIZTECH. Operator and support contact: KEMIZTECH, adam89wood@gmail.com. See the Privacy Policy for how subscription and advertising data is handled.

1. What's free, what's paid

Core recording, offline speech-to-text transcription using a model you've downloaded, transcript editing and essential text/subtitle export are available without a subscription for as long as you use the app. Recognition accuracy depends on your recording, chosen language, model and device, and transcription is not guaranteed to be error-free. The optional subscription removes in-app advertising while it is active; it does not change transcription quality or add features beyond ad removal.

2. Plans and pricing

Keep My Word currently offers:

  • Monthly — A$1.49 per month (indicative price; the store you purchase through always displays the final localized price, any applicable taxes and the exact billing interval before you pay).
  • Yearly — A$9.99 per year, under the same terms.

No free trial is offered. Prices may be adjusted over time; any change will be reflected in the store listing and will not retroactively change a period you've already paid for.

3. Billing, renewal and cancellation

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play (Google Play edition) or the Amazon Appstore (Amazon Appstore edition) using the account you're signed into on that store. KEMIZTECH never receives your payment card details. Subscriptions renew automatically at the price and interval you agreed to until you cancel. Cancelling stops future renewal; your ad-free access continues until the end of the period already paid for, as confirmed by the store. Refunds and billing disputes are handled under the applicable store's own refund rules and consumer law, not directly by KEMIZTECH. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription — cancel it from Google Play or the Amazon Appstore account settings.

4. How access is verified

Purchasing, restoring and periodically re-confirming a subscription requires internet access so the app can check your receipt against Google Play or Amazon and receive a signed confirmation from our verification service. A previously verified subscription keeps working offline for up to 72 hours (some Amazon receipts refresh hourly); if it cannot be refreshed before that window ends, ads may resume until the app reconnects, but your local recordings, transcripts and offline transcription are never affected. Restoring a subscription on a new device or after reinstalling requires signing into the same store account used to purchase it. Google Play and Amazon purchases are managed separately by each store and do not transfer between them.

5. Rewarded video

Keep My Word separately offers an optional rewarded video, provided by Google AdMob, that grants 30 minutes without ads once the ad SDK confirms it was watched to completion. This is a free, one-off perk, not a purchase or subscription; it does not renew and ends when the app's process is restarted. A video may not always be available, and this never blocks offline transcription.
