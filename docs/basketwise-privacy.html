Basketwise Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 November 2026.

Basketwise is a grocery price comparison app published by KemizTech.

Information stored on your device

Basketwise stores your saved deals, shopping list and selected area on your device. The app does not require an account or send your shopping list or saved deals to the price server. You can clear your list in the app and remove local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Catalogue requests

When the price service is available, Basketwise requests current catalogue prices. The server receives your IP address and standard request information to respond. It requests catalogue data from the price provider separately.

Advertising and privacy choices

Android builds with advertising use Google Mobile Ads and Google's consent tools. Google may process device identifiers, IP address, app interactions and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads. Where available, you can review your advertising privacy choices in the app. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.
